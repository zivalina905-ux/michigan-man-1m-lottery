Loto Cash: Puerto Rico's flagship jackpot game

How to play: pick five numbers from 1–35 plus one CASH number from 1–10 — $1 per play. Draws run Monday, Wednesday and Friday. The jackpot starts at $500,000 and grows $10,000 per drawing without a winner.

  • Revancha ("Doble Revancha," +$1): your numbers get a second automatic redraw — a built-in second chance at the jackpot.
  • Multiplier (+$1): scales non-jackpot prizes up to 5X.
  • Recent wins: a $570,000 jackpot hit in September 2026 on an automatic-play ticket sold at Canas Shell Service, Ponce — and an $860,000 jackpot was claimed earlier in the year (Telemundo Puerto Rico coverage).
  • Payout: "un solo pago" — the advertised jackpot is a single lump sum paid at Lotería Electrónica headquarters, not an annuity.

Only your tax liability on prizes over $10,000 reduces the payout — no U.S.-style 24% federal withholding at the counter (see the tax section below).

Wild Ball — Puerto Rico's PA-style add-on: every Pega 2/3/4 drawing also produces a fifth Wild Ball number. Players who bought the option can substitute the Wild Ball for any drawn digit to create winning combinations — example: winning numbers 1-2-3 with Wild Ball 5 makes 5-2-3, 1-5-3 and 1-2-5 all winners on a Pega 3 Exacta play. Adding Wild Ball doubles the ticket cost. Pennsylvania's near-identical add-on is covered in our PA Lottery Pick 4 guide.

Aproximaciones (+50¢ per play): win if your number is exactly one digit higher or lower than the drawn number — Michigan's 1-Off bet works the same way.

Sold-out rule: like Kentucky, Puerto Rico suspends sales of a specific number once its total payable liability hits $10M (Pega 3) or $15M (Pega 4) — popular numbers can genuinely sell out.

Keno & instant games

  • Keno: pick 1–10 numbers of 80, $1–$20 per draw, drawings every 4 minutes Monday–Saturday 5 a.m.–2 a.m., up to 20 consecutive draws per playslip, 37 ways to win (from matching 10-of-10 down to matching none).
  • Instantáneos (scratch-offs): $1, $2, $3, $5 and $10 cards; top prizes from $5,000 up to $150,000 on the $10 games.
  • Powerball & Mega Millions: sold island-wide; advance play covers 2–12 consecutive Powerball draws.

Keno's 4-minute cadence mirrors Puerto Rico's island rhythm — like Kentucky's Keno, it's a scanner-check game, not an eyeball-the-results-page game.

Powerball via MUSL: the 2014 milestone

In 2014, Puerto Rico became the first Spanish-speaking jurisdiction to join MUSL (the Multi-State Lottery Association), putting the island in the Powerball consortium — same tickets, same odds (1 in 292,201,338 for the jackpot), same billion-dollar pools as the states. Mega Millions followed.

The jackpot formats differ from Loto Cash: Powerball and Mega Millions winners choose annuity or lump sum — the same fork analyzed in our Brenda Aubin-Vega annuity-vs-lump-sum breakdown and taken as cash by Georgia's record $983M winner.

All Lotería Electrónica prizes are free of contributions up to $10,000 — meaning the vast majority of Pega and Keno wins cost nothing at tax time. A $570,000 Loto Cash jackpot's first $10,000 is tax-free; the remainder carries tax liability. Compare with U.S. states in our guides: Michigan 4.25%, Pennsylvania 3.07%, Kentucky 3.5%, Georgia 4.99%, Ohio 4% withholding.

One caveat: U.S. mainland residents who win in Puerto Rico may still owe home-state tax — cross-border lottery tax questions are CPA territory.

Puerto Rico lottery — FAQ

What games does the Puerto Rico lottery offer?
Lotería Electrónica runs Loto Cash (jackpot from $500,000), Pega 2, Pega 3 and Pega 4 digit games drawn twice daily, Keno every four minutes, instant scratch-offs up to $150,000, Cash POP-style instant games, plus Powerball and Mega Millions through MUSL membership.
How do you play Loto Cash in Puerto Rico?
Pick five numbers from 1-35 plus one CASH number from 1-10, $1 per play. Draws are Monday, Wednesday and Friday. The jackpot starts at $500,000 and grows $10,000 per roll. Add Revancha for $1 (a second-chance redraw) or the multiplier for $1 (scales minor prizes up to 5X). Recent jackpots: $570,000 won in Ponce (September 2026) and $860,000 claimed earlier in the year.
When are the Puerto Rico Pega drawings?
Pega 2, Pega 3 and Pega 4 draw Monday through Saturday at 2:00 p.m. and 10:00 p.m., broadcast live on WIPR Canal 6. The Wild Ball add-on draws a fifth number after each game that can substitute for any drawn digit, doubling the ticket cost.
Are Puerto Rico lottery winnings taxed?
All Lotería Electrónica prizes are tax-free up to $10,000. Larger prizes carry tax liability on the amount over $10,000 — a major difference from U.S. states, where federal withholding starts at $5,000. A $570,000 Loto Cash jackpot's first $10,000 is tax-free; the rest is taxable income.
How long do you have to claim a Puerto Rico lottery prize?
180 days. Prizes under $1,200 can be claimed at any authorized retailer; $1,200-$25,000 at Hacienda colecturías (Carolina, Coamo, Guayama, Santurce, Yauco, Vega Baja, San Sebastián); over $25,000 at Lotería Electrónica headquarters in Hato Rey (Edificio Fundación Ángel Ramos, 383 Ave. F.D. Roosevelt, San Juan).
Does Puerto Rico play Powerball and Mega Millions?
Yes. Puerto Rico joined MUSL in 2014 — the first Spanish-speaking jurisdiction in the Powerball consortium — so the same Powerball tickets sold in the states are sold on the island. Mega Millions is also available. Advance play covers 2-12 consecutive draws.
Can I watch the Pega drawings live?
Yes. Pega drawings broadcast live on WIPR Canal 6, and the public can attend in person at Canal 6 studios by coordinating with Lotería Electrónica's Security Section at (787) 250-8150, ext. 5860-5862.
