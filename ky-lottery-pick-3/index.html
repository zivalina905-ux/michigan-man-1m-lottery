Kentucky Pick 3 — quick answer

Quick answer Kentucky Pick 3 draws twice daily — Midday 1:20 p.m., Evening 11:00 p.m. ET — using a random number generator. Pick three digits 0–9, wager 50¢ up to $5, and win fixed prizes: $600 on a $1 Straight (1 in 1,000), up to $3,000 on a $5 Straight. Seven play types including Super Straight (any-order, full prize) and Split Pair (first + last digit only). Numbers can sell out under the Lottery's $1.5M-per-draw liability cap. How to play KY Lottery Pick 3

  1. Pick three digits, 0–9.

    Duplicates and leading zeros count — 0-0-7 and 5-5-5 are valid plays. Or ask for a Quick Pick.

  2. Choose your wager: 50¢ to $5.

    Kentucky is one of the few states letting you bet up to $5 per play — prizes scale linearly ($1 Straight $600 → $5 Straight $3,000).

  3. Choose a play type.

    Straight, Box, Straight/Box, Super Straight, Front Pair, Back Pair, or Split Pair — all detailed below.

  4. Select Midday, Evening, or advance plays.

    Multi-draw covers future consecutive drawings on one ticket.

  5. Check and sign immediately.

    Scan at the retailer or in the KY Lottery app; sign the back — an unsigned ticket is a bearer instrument anyone can cash.

🎓
Where the money goes: about two-thirds of Kentucky ticket revenue pays prizes; roughly a fifth goes to the Commonwealth — funding college scholarship and grant programs (KY's version of education funding, like Michigan's School Aid Fund). The rest covers retailer commissions and administration.

Kentucky's distinctive bet types

  • Straight — exact order, the top prize at every wager level.
  • Box — any order; 3-way (two like digits) pays double the 6-way (all different).
  • Straight/Box — fixed $1: exact order pays both halves, other covered orders pay the Box half.
  • Super Straight — Kentucky's name for a Combo: every arrangement entered as a separate Straight, so any order wins the full $600-per-$1. The cost scales with your digits (3-way: $3 per $1 unit; 6-way: $6) — the any-order bet that never discounts a win. Michigan calls the same play a Wheel bet.
  • Front Pair / Back Pair — match just the first two or last two digits in order, 1 in 100 odds.
  • Split Pair — Kentucky-exclusive: matches the first and last digits in order, ignoring the middle. Play 5-?-7 and 5-0-7, 5-9-7, 5-x-7 all win $60 on $1. Same odds and prize as the other pairs — just a different slice of the number.
  • 1-Off — discontinued: the near-miss bet (win when one digit is ±1) was removed in January 2021. Michigan still runs its version — see the MI 1-Off guide.

Why KY Pick 3 numbers sell out — the $1.5M liability cap

Kentucky is one of the few states where a lottery number can literally sell out. The mechanics:

  • The Kentucky Lottery holds $1.5 million of prize liability per drawing.
  • If players buy a popular combination heavily enough that paying it out would breach the cap — 777 is the classic case — the terminal suspends sales of that specific number until the next draw.
  • It's a solvency control, not a conspiracy: every ticket already sold remains guaranteed. But the lesson is real — triple-digit repeats and date patterns (077, 1225) sell out fastest, and if you want them, buy early.

No other state in our coverage does this — South Carolina and Pennsylvania instead rely on fixed-prize tables that pay every winner in full regardless of how many there are.

KY lottery Pick 3 — FAQ

What time is the KY Lottery Pick 3 drawing?
Kentucky Pick 3 draws twice daily: a Midday drawing at 1:20 p.m. and an Evening drawing at 11:00 p.m. Eastern Time. Ticket sales close shortly before each draw, and results post on kylottery.com minutes later. Numbers are selected by a random number generator, not ball machines.
How much does KY Pick 3 pay?
A $1 Straight pays $600 for matching all three digits in exact order — 1 in 1,000 odds. Wagers run from 50 cents to $5, so a $5 Straight pays $3,000. Box pays $200 (3-way) or $100 (6-way) on $1, Front/Back/Split Pair pays $60, and Straight/Box pays up to $400 combined on an exact match.
What is Super Straight in Kentucky Pick 3?
Super Straight is Kentucky's version of a Combo play: every arrangement of your three digits is entered as a separate Straight bet, so any order wins the full $600-per-$1 Straight prize. A 3-way Super Straight costs $3 per $1 unit; a 6-way costs $6. It's the any-order bet that never discounts your win.
What is a Split Pair in KY Pick 3?
Split Pair is a Kentucky-exclusive play type: it matches the first and last digits in order, ignoring the middle digit. Play 5-?-7 and any draw starting with 5 and ending with 7 wins $60 on a $1 wager — the same prize and odds (1 in 100) as Front Pair and Back Pair.
Why do some KY Pick 3 numbers get sold out?
The Kentucky Lottery caps its prize liability at $1.5 million per drawing. If enough players buy a popular combination — 777 is the classic example — that selling it could push potential payouts past the cap, so sales of that specific number are suspended. It's a liability control, not a scam: your ticket, once sold, is guaranteed.
How long do I have to claim a KY Pick 3 prize?
180 days from the drawing date. Prizes up to $600 can be claimed at any Kentucky Lottery retailer without a form. Larger prizes require a Prize Claim form and photo ID — claim at an authorized cashing agent, by mail to the Kentucky Lottery Claims Department (1011 W Main St., Louisville, KY 40202), or at headquarters in Louisville for claims over $25,000.
How much tax does Kentucky withhold on Pick 3 winnings?
Kentucky's flat income tax is 3.5% for 2026 (down from 4%), withheld on winnings over $5,000, plus 24% federal withholding. A $3,000 maximum Pick 3 prize falls under both thresholds — no automatic withholding — but it's still taxable income you report at filing time.
