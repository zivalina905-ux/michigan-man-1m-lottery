How to play Daily 3 & Daily 4

  1. Pick your game and digits.

    Daily 3: three digits 0–9. Daily 4: four digits. Duplicates and leading zeros count — 0-0-7 and 1-1-1-2 are valid plays. Or play a Quick Pick.

  2. Choose your wager.

    Daily 3: 50¢ or $1 per bet. Daily 4: $1 per bet. $1 bets pay double the 50¢ prizes.

  3. Choose a bet type.

    Straight, Box, Wheel, 2-Way, or 1-Off — Michigan's full menu is bigger than most states'. Details below.

  4. Pick Midday, Evening, or both — up to 60 draws.

    Mark the multiple-draw area for consecutive drawings on one ticket.

  5. Check and sign immediately.

    Scan at the retailer or in the Michigan Lottery app; sign the back before anything else. An unsigned ticket is a bearer instrument — whoever holds it can claim it — so a signed ticket is the only claim-proof that exists.

🎓
Where the money goes: Daily 3 and Daily 4 profits fund Michigan public education (K-12 School Aid Fund) — the same beneficiary behind every $1M winner story on our homepage. Retailers earn commission on tickets sold and cashed.

Daily 4 vs. other states' Pick 4: Michigan's $5,000 Straight matches Pennsylvania's $5,000 and Maryland's $5,000 exactly — but Michigan's Wheel bet is the differentiator: any-order wins pay the full Straight prize ($5,000), because Wheel buys every combination as separate Straight entries.

The five Michigan bet types — including 1-Off

  • Straight — your digits in exact order. Cleanest bet, top prize.
  • Box — any order. Fewer unique arrangements = bigger prize: 1-1-2 has three orders (3-way), 1-2-3 has six (6-way).
  • Wheel — every arrangement of your digits entered as separate Straight bets, so any order wins the full Straight prize. Costs more (one unit per combination) but never discounts your win.
  • 2-Way — splits your wager: half on Straight, half on Box. Exact order pays both halves; any other covered order pays the Box half.
  • 1-Off — Michigan's signature bet, absent in most states. Your numbers win if they match exactly OR if exactly one digit is one number higher or lower than drawn. Play 2-5-8 and six different draws pay: 2-5-9, 2-5-7, 2-6-8, 2-4-8, 3-5-8, 1-5-8 — plus the exact match. The $284/$36 prize tiers price in that expanded coverage.

1-Off strategy note: it's built for players who "almost" hit — the near-miss tier turns one-wrong-digit heartbreak into a small cash win. It costs the same 50¢/$1 as any other bet; the trade is a lower top prize ($284 vs $500 on Daily 3) for six extra winning combinations.

3 digit 4 digit Michigan lottery — FAQ

What are Michigan's 3 digit and 4 digit lottery games?
Daily 3 and Daily 4 — Michigan's versions of the Pick 3 and Pick 4 games played in most states. Daily 3 uses three digits (0-9) with a $500 top prize on a $1 Straight; Daily 4 uses four digits with a $5,000 top prize. Both draw twice daily: Midday at 12:59 p.m. and Evening at 7:29 p.m. ET.
What time is the Michigan Daily 3 and Daily 4 drawing?
Both games draw every day — Midday at 12:59 p.m. and Evening at 7:29 p.m. Eastern Time. Ticket sales close at 12:39 p.m. for Midday and 7:08 p.m. for Evening, with a two-minute break before sales resume for the next draw.
What bet types does Michigan Daily 3 and Daily 4 offer?
Five bet types: Straight (exact order), Box (any order), Wheel (every combination entered as separate Straights), 2-Way (splits the wager between Straight and Box), and 1-Off — Michigan's signature bet that wins even if one digit is one number up or down from the drawn result.
How much does Michigan Daily 4 pay?
A $1 Straight pays $5,000 (1 in 10,000). Box pays $1,250 (4-way), $833 (6-way), $416 (12-way) or $208 (24-way). Wheel pays $5,000 on any-order wins, Straight/Box pays up to $3,200 exact, and 1-Off pays $2,800 on a $1 play.
How long do I have to claim a Michigan Daily 3 or Daily 4 prize?
One year (365 days) from the drawing date. As of August 2026, retailers and self-serve machines can cash prizes up to $999.99 — up from the old $600 limit. Larger prizes go to Lottery Claim Centers (Lansing, Livonia, Sterling Heights, Detroit, Grand Rapids, Saginaw) by appointment, partner banks in northern Michigan and the U.P., or by mail to Lansing.
What is the 1-Off bet in Michigan Daily 3 and Daily 4?
Michigan's signature bet type: your numbers win if they match the drawing exactly, OR if exactly one digit is one number higher or lower than drawn. Play 2-5-8 and a draw of 2-5-9, 2-5-7, 2-6-8, 2-4-8, 3-5-8 or 1-5-8 all pay — a $1 Daily 3 1-Off pays $284, Daily 4 pays $2,800.
How much does Michigan tax Daily 3 and Daily 4 winnings?
Michigan withholds 4.25% state tax on prizes over $5,000, plus 24% federal withholding. A $5,000 Daily 4 Straight sits right at that threshold. Smaller prizes — most Daily 3 and Daily 4 wins — are still taxable income you report at filing time, but nothing is withheld up front.
