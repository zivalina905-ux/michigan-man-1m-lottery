The 90-day scratcher trap: Georgia instant games expire 90 days after the game ends — not after purchase. Check the game's end date on galottery.com; a ticket bought early in a game's life can quietly sit months past its deadline. Unclaimed Georgia prizes don't roll into future jackpots — unclaimed funds help fund programs, per statute.

Georgia's 180/90 windows are tight compared to the neighbors we've covered — Maryland's 182-day Pick games, Oklahoma's 180-day Powerball, and New York's full one-year claim period. Can Georgia lottery winners stay anonymous?

Yes — and Georgia's biggest winner proved it. Georgia law permits large-prize winners to keep their identities private, and the Georgia Lottery releases names and cities only with the winner's consent. That's why the $983M record holder is known only as "a Georgia resident whose family benefited from the HOPE Scholarship." The same privacy-friendly state runs GA lottery iLottery — the official online play platform — where digital wins up to $600 land straight in your account.

That's a meaningful contrast for readers in states without such protections. In Michigan, for example, big winners' names, cities and games become public record — our homepage's Michigan $1M winner stories are all attributable. Where Georgia lottery money goes: HOPE Scholarship lottery funding & Pre-K

The Georgia Lottery was created by the Lottery for Education Act (codified in OCGA Title 50, Chapter 27), and its entire profit mission is education funding: the HOPE Scholarship (tuition for Georgia students), HOPE Grant, and the universal Pre-K program. Billions have flowed to Georgia students since 1993 — including, ironically, the family of the anonymous $983M winner.

Even unclaimed prizes serve public purposes: under O.C.G.A. § 50-27-24(h), up to $200,000 of unclaimed prize money each year goes to the Department of Behavioral Health for compulsive-gambling treatment and education, with the rest available for future prize pools and promotions.

The full-circle detail from the record claim bears repeating: the winner said they regularly played to support education programs — and their own family had been HOPE recipients. Every Georgia lottery winning story is, one way or another, an education-funding story too.

Georgia lottery winning — FAQ

What is the biggest Georgia lottery winning ever?
A $983 million Mega Millions jackpot from the Nov. 14, 2025 drawing — Georgia's largest lottery win in history. The winner came forward on Jan. 2, 2026, chose the $453.6 million cash option before taxes, and remained anonymous under Georgia law. The Quik Pik ticket was sold at a Publix on North Highway 29 in Newnan, which earned a $50,000 retailer bonus.
How long do I have to claim a Georgia lottery prize?
Under Georgia law (O.C.G.A. 50-27-24), draw-game and multistate prizes must be claimed within 180 days of the drawing, while instant-game (scratcher) prizes must be claimed within 90 days of the game's end — unless the game participates in a multistate instant game, which carries 180 days.
Can you stay anonymous after winning the Georgia Lottery?
Yes. Georgia law allows big-jackpot winners to remain anonymous — the $983M Mega Millions winner did exactly that. The Georgia Lottery only releases identifying information with the winner's consent, which is why Georgia's record winner is known only as a resident whose family benefited from the HOPE Scholarship.
How much tax is withheld on a Georgia lottery winning?
Prizes over $5,000 face automatic withholding: 24% federal plus Georgia's flat 4.99% state income tax. On the $983M jackpot's $453.6M cash option, withholding alone exceeded $130 million — and the winner's final bill is squared up at filing time.
What are the odds of winning Fantasy 5 in Georgia?
Fantasy 5 jackpot odds are 1 in 850,668 — far better than Powerball's 1 in 292,201,338. The $1 game draws daily at 11:34 p.m. ET, the jackpot starts at $125,000 and rolls until won, and an optional Ca$h Match add-on costs $1 more.
Can you buy Georgia Lottery tickets online?
Yes — Georgia is one of the states with full online play through its official iLottery: draw games, scratchers-style Diggi games, and online prize payouts up to $600 credited straight to your account. You must be a Georgia resident or physically located in the state to play online.
Where does Georgia Lottery money go?
To the HOPE Scholarship and Pre-K programs. Every Georgia lottery winning — and every losing ticket — funds tuition assistance for Georgia students. Even the record $983M winner said they regularly played to support education programs, and their family had benefited from the HOPE Scholarship.
