A $6.5 million Pick-6 jackpot, won from a phone

The New Jersey Lottery confirmed that a single ticket — purchased through the Jackpocket mobile app — matched all six numbers in the Monday, March 23, 2026 drawing to win the $6.5 million Pick-6 jackpot.

"Congratulations to our latest Pick-6 winner!" said NJ Lottery Executive Director James Carey. "This is the second Pick-6 jackpot this year."

The win minted New Jersey's newest millionaire without that person ever walking into a store. The jackpot reset to $2 million for the next drawing (Thursday, March 26).

The winning numbers

Winning Pick-6 numbers — Monday, March 23, 2026

02 · 03 · 06 · 33 · 38 · 41

Six numbers, one ticket, one match on all six — and $6.5 million changed hands. Pick-6 asks players to choose six numbers from 1 to 46; matching all six in a single drawing wins or shares the jackpot.

Jackpocket: how the app ticket worked

Jackpocket is a licensed lottery courier app: it buys official tickets on a customer's behalf from authorized New Jersey Lottery retailers, stores a scanned image in the user's account, and notifies the user of any win. The physical ticket lives with the courier; the win is the customer's.

  • Order: the player picked six numbers (or Quick Picked) in the app and paid $2 per play.
  • Purchase: Jackpocket placed the order with an authorized NJ Lottery retailer.
  • Match: all six numbers hit in the March 23 drawing — a $6.5 million jackpot.
  • Notification: the app alerted the customer; big-prize claims route through the Lottery with the courier's cooperation.

New Jersey is one of the states where courier-app play is legal and regulated — which is why a phone-first jackpot like this is possible there. Not every state allows it: Michigan runs its own official online sales but doesn't use third-party couriers, and several states ban them outright.

The jackpot odds — 1 in 9,366,819 — are roughly 31× better than Powerball's (1 in 292,201,338) and about 8× better than Mega Millions'. That's the pitch of state lotto games: smaller jackpots, dramatically better odds. A $2 million starting jackpot rolls every drawing until someone hits 6-of-6 — which is exactly how it reached $6.5 million by late March.

XTRA: the built-in multiplier

Every $2 Pick-6 ticket includes a random XTRA multiplier — 1X, 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X or 10X — applied to all non-jackpot prizes:

  • A $1,500 five-number match can become $15,000 with a 10X multiplier.
  • A $30 four-number match can become $300.
  • The jackpot itself is never multiplied.

XTRA is included in the base $2 price — no add-on purchase needed. It's the same concept as Powerball's Power Play or Mega Millions' Megaplier, just bundled by default.

Taxes: New Jersey is unusual — the state does not tax lottery winnings, so nothing is withheld for NJ income tax. The IRS does: 24% federal withholding on prizes over $5,000, with the final bill at filing (top bracket 37%). On a $6.5 million jackpot, that's about $1.56 million withheld at claim — and the winner's actual federal liability is computed annually.

Jackpot payout choice: like most lotto games, Pick-6 winners choose between the advertised annuity (paid over decades) or a reduced one-time cash value. The $6.5M figure is the annuitized headline; the cash option is lower.
