Maryland Pick 3 Midday and Maryland Pick 4 Evening are two separate sessions — most players run one in the afternoon and the other at night, or the same digits in both. The cutoff logic matches every other two-session state we've covered — South Carolina's 12:59/6:59 and Pennsylvania's 1:35/6:59: sales stop hard, a ticket bought at 12:25 plays the next session, and the one-minute weekend shift (12:27 → 12:28) is Maryland's signature quirk. How to play Maryland Pick 3 and Pick 4

  1. Pick your game — three or four digits.

    Pick 3: three digits, each 0–9. Pick 4: four digits. Leading zeros count — 0-7-3 is a full Pick 3 play, 0-1-1-2 a full Pick 4 play, never "73" or "112".

  2. Choose a wager: 50¢ or $1.

    All prizes scale ×2 at 50 cents. Pick 3 bets cost 50¢–$5 per play; Pick 4 the same.

  3. Choose a play type.

    Straight, Box, Straight/Box, or Pairs (Pick 3: Front/Back Pair; Pick 4: Front/Back Pair; Pick 5 also runs 3-Digit/4-Digit partial bets). Each table is below.

  4. Choose the draw time — and how many.

    Midday, Evening, or both (multi-draw / advance play). Counting panels × sessions × days before paying — three $1 plays on both Maryland sessions run $6 a day.

  5. Verify the printed ticket, then sign it.

    The printed ticket — not the playslip or app draft — is the only valid entry. Sign the back immediately: an unsigned Pick ticket is a bearer instrument.

Maryland's 182-day window is stricter than neighboring Pennsylvania's one-year clock but matches Oklahoma and South Carolina. The Pick 3/Pick 4 deadline is from the drawing date, not the purchase date — check your ticket carefully, especially on multi-draw plays.

Taxes: Maryland withholds ~8.75% state on larger prizes plus 24% federal — a $5,000 Pick 4 Straight loses about $1,637 in withholding. Like Georgia's 4.99% rate and Puerto Rico's tax-free-under-$10K rule, the true-up at filing depends on your total income.

The two-date trap: write both dates on your calendar — the purchase date (when multi-draw play began) and the drawing date (when the 182-day clock actually starts). Expired tickets go to the state, not back to you.

Pick 3 Pick 4 Maryland lottery — FAQ

What time is the Maryland Pick 3 and Pick 4 drawing?
Maryland draws Pick 3 and Pick 4 twice daily: Midday at 12:27 p.m. ET Monday–Friday (12:28 p.m. weekends), with sales closing at 12:24 p.m., and Evening at 7:56 p.m. ET Monday–Saturday (8:10 p.m. Sundays), with sales closing at 7:52 p.m. (8:05 p.m. Sundays). Tickets bought after a cutoff play the next drawing.
How much does Maryland Pick 3 pay?
A $1 Straight Pick 3 bet pays $500 for matching all three digits in exact order ($250 on a 50-cent play). Box pays $160 for a 3-way match or $80 for a 6-way match. Front Pair and Back Pair each pay $50 on a $1 play. All prizes are fixed.
How much does Maryland Pick 4 pay?
A $1 Straight Pick 4 bet pays $5,000 for matching all four digits in exact order ($2,500 on 50 cents). Box prizes scale by digit arrangement: $1,200 for a 4-way, $800 for a 6-way, $400 for a 12-way, and $200 for a 24-way match on a $1 play. All prizes are fixed.
How long do I have to claim a Maryland Pick 3 or Pick 4 prize?
182 days from the drawing date — the standard deadline for all Maryland draw games. Retailers pay prizes up to $600 on the spot; larger prizes go to Maryland Lottery claim centers or headquarters, or can be claimed by mail with the ticket and claim form.
How is Maryland Pick 3/Pick 4 different from Pick 5?
Pick 5 adds a fifth digit to the format — a $1 Straight pays $50,000 (versus $500 for Pick 3 and $5,000 for Pick 4) with odds of 1 in 100,000, and its pair bets are Front 3/Back 3 and Front 4/Back 4 in exact order. Pick 5 is covered in the Midday guide; Pick 3 and Pick 4 share the same twice-daily schedule and the same 182-day claim rule.
Where does Maryland Lottery money go?
To the state — Maryland Lottery proceeds flow to the General Fund, funding education and other state programs. Like Michigan's School Aid Fund and Kentucky's KEES scholarships, every losing ticket still contributes.
