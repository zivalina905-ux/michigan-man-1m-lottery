NY lottery results today — quick answer

Quick answer New York's three daily-digit games — Numbers (3 digits), Win 4 (4 digits) and Take 5 (5 numbers from 1–39) — draw twice a day: Midday ≈2:30 p.m., Evening ≈10:30 p.m. ET, with sales closing at 2:15 p.m. and 10:20 p.m. All NY lottery results today — the new York Numbers results, Win 4 results, and Take 5 results — post on nylottery.ny.gov minutes after each draw (also streamed live), and mirrors like Lottery Post republish them. Straight prizes: Numbers $500, Win 4 $5,000 on $1; Take 5's pari-mutuel jackpot pays 1-in-575,757. Draw-game tickets expire one year from the drawing date — checking your numbers doesn't pause that clock.

Where do NY Lottery results post first?

"Lottery Post" is the name of a popular results site — but the source of truth is always the official operator. Here's the checking hierarchy, best-first:

  • nylottery.ny.gov (official) — the new York lottery winning numbers page by game and date, past-results archive, and the live drawing stream. First place numbers are certified.
  • The official New York Lottery app — scans your physical ticket and returns an instant yes/no verdict, session by session.
  • Any NY Lottery retailer scanner — free, instant, and it validates the ticket's barcode rather than your eyes.
  • Results mirrors (Lottery Post, LotteryUSA, Lottery.net) — fast and convenient for browsing NY lottery post archives, but always same-session-same-game; a mirror's "latest" can briefly show the other session.
🛡️
Anti-scam rule: never post a readable ticket barcode online when asking "did I win?" — describe the game, session and play type instead. A barcode photo is all a thief needs.

Checking N.Y. lottery post results the right way

Most "I thought I won" confusion is a checking error, not a lottery error. The five-point checklist that prevents it:

  1. Match the game name first.

    Numbers ≠ Win 4 ≠ Take 5. Three digits inside a Win 4 result don't settle a Numbers ticket — each game is checked against its own drawing.

  2. Match the session — Midday or Evening.

    The session is printed on your ticket. Comparing a Midday ticket to Evening numbers is the single most common false-win mistake.

  3. Match the date — especially on advance plays.

    A combination drawn last week or in another session is history, not a win. Each purchased drawing is checked on its own date.

  4. Honor the play type.

    Straight demands exact order. Box accepts any covered order. Pairs check only their named positions. Leading zeros count — 0-7-3 is a full three-digit play.

  5. Check Instant Win separately.

    The $1 feature has its own printed numbers, columns and prize table — it's a different game layer than the Midday/Evening result.

One habit beats every other on this page: scan the physical ticket at a retailer or in the official app instead of eyeballing numbers. The scanner validates the barcode and never misreads a session.

N.Y. lottery post results — FAQ

What time are the N.Y. Lottery drawings posted?
Numbers and Win 4 draw twice daily at approximately 2:30 p.m. Midday and 10:30 p.m. Evening Eastern Time; Take 5 draws at the same two times. Sales close at 2:15 p.m. and 10:20 p.m. Results post on nylottery.ny.gov shortly after each drawing, and every drawing is streamed live.
Where can I check NY Lottery results?
The official source is nylottery.ny.gov — winning numbers page, past-results archives, and the live drawing stream. Independent mirrors like Lottery Post and LotteryUSA republish the same numbers. Any NY Lottery retailer can scan a ticket, and the official app checks tickets directly.
How much does NY Numbers pay?
A $1 Straight Numbers play pays $500 (1 in 1,000). A 3-Way Box pays $160, a 6-Way Box pays $80, and Front/Back Pair pays $50 — all fixed prizes on a $1 play, half that at 50 cents.
How much does NY Win 4 pay?
A $1 Straight Win 4 play pays $5,000 (1 in 10,000). Box prizes run from $1,200 (4-Way) down to $200 (24-Way) on a $1 play, and Front/Back Pair pays $50. Straight/Box pays up to $3,100 on an exact 4-Way match.
What is Take 5 and what are its odds?
Take 5 is New York's $1 daily lotto: pick 5 numbers from 1 to 39, drawn twice daily. Jackpot odds are 1 in 575,757 with a pari-mutuel (shared) top prize; matching 3 of 5 pays a shared prize at 1 in 102.63, and 2 of 5 wins a free Quick Pick.
What is Instant Win on NY Numbers and Win 4 tickets?
A $1-per-play add-on that prints separate instant matching numbers and prizes up to $500 per match on the ticket itself. The overall chance of some instant prize is 1 in 5.30 — mostly small amounts — and it never affects the scheduled Midday or Evening drawing.
How long do I have to claim a NY Lottery prize?
One year (365 days) from the drawing date for draw games. Prizes under $600 can be claimed at retailers, customer service centers, prize claim centers or by mail; $600 and up requires a claim form and ID. Some casino-based claim centers require visitors to be 21.
