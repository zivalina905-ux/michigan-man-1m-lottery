Six play types — Straight, Box, Straight/Box, Combo, Front Pair, Back Pair — top prize $600 on a $1 Straight. Wild Ball doubles cost for extra combinations; EZ Match adds instant wins. Missouri Pick 3 Midday and Missouri Pick 3 Evening run back-to-back every day — the sales cutoff comes first, then the draw break. Tickets bought after a cutoff automatically play the next drawing. You can enter Midday, Evening, or both, and play up to 14 consecutive draws on one playslip.

The rhythm matches South Carolina Pick 3 (12:59/6:59) and Michigan Daily 3 (12:59/7:29) — but Missouri's late Evening window (buy until 8:59) is one of the latest cutoff windows in the country.

How to play MO Lottery Pick 3

  1. Pick three digits, 000–999.

    Duplicates and leading zeros count — 0-0-7 and 7-7-7 are valid plays. Or take a Quick Pick.

  2. Choose a play type — Missouri has six.

    Straight, Box, Straight/Box, Combo, Front Pair, Back Pair — plus two optional add-ons (Wild Ball, EZ Match) below.

  3. Bet 50¢ or $1.

    All prizes double at $1 versus 50¢. Five play boards per playslip.

  4. Pick Midday, Evening, or both — up to 14 draws.

    Multi-draw covers consecutive drawings on a single playslip.

  5. Verify the printed ticket and sign it.

    Game, digits, play type, wager, draw selection, Wild Ball, EZ Match — verify everything before leaving the counter. An unsigned ticket is a bearer instrument — possession is the claim.

EZ Match: instant wins before the draw

EZ Match is Missouri's instant add-on (similar to Michigan's EZmatch): mark it on your playslip, and the terminal prints a separate set of EZ Match numbers with cash prizes attached. Match any EZ Match number to any of your Pick 3 numbers and you win on the spot — an EZ Match instant prize, no waiting for the Midday or Evening draw.

  • Instant prizes range up to a few thousand dollars depending on the game's current prize table.
  • Adding EZ Match increases the cost per play — check the ticket total before paying.
  • An EZ Match win is paid at the retailer; your base Pick 3 play still runs in the drawing.

Missouri Pick 3 sold out: the $1.5M liability cap

Missouri caps Pick 3 liability at $1.5 million per drawing. Every prize is fixed, so the Lottery tracks sales of each three-digit combination — and when a popular number (the classics: 123, 777, 911) would breach the cap, sales of that number are suspended until the next draw.

What it means for players:

  • It's rare — mostly on "birthday-friendly" or repeating-digit combinations around big jackpot runs.
  • Tickets already sold remain guaranteed — fixed prizes pay in full regardless of winner count.
  • The workaround is simple: play a different number, or wait for the next draw. There's no market where you can buy the sold-out combination secondhand.

MO lottery Pick 3 — FAQ

What time is the Missouri Pick 3 drawing?
Missouri draws Pick 3 twice daily. Ticket sales close at 12:45 p.m. for the Midday draw (draw break until 12:59 p.m.) and 8:59 p.m. for the Evening draw (draw break until 9:15 p.m.). Tickets bought after a cutoff play the next drawing.
How much does MO Lottery Pick 3 pay?
The top prize is $600 for a $1 Straight play matching all three digits in exact order ($300 on a 50-cent play). Box pays $200 (3-way) or $100 (6-way), Straight/Box pays up to $400 combined, Pairs pay $60, and Combo plays pay full Straight prizes per combination.
What play types does Missouri Pick 3 offer?
Six play types: Straight (exact order), Box (any order — 3-way or 6-way), Straight/Box (half on each), Combo (every arrangement as a separate Straight), Front Pair and Back Pair. Wild Ball and EZ Match are optional add-ons on top of all of these.
How does the Wild Ball work in Missouri Pick 3?
Wild Ball is an optional add-on that doubles the ticket cost. A fourth number from 0-9 is drawn, and Wild Ball players may swap any one of the three drawn digits for it. A $1 Straight with a Wild Ball match pays $200 instead of $600 — more ways to win, smaller side prizes.
Why do some Missouri Pick 3 numbers sell out?
Missouri caps Pick 3 liability at $1.5 million per draw. If enough players buy a popular combination — 123, 777, 911 are the classics — selling more would exceed what the Lottery can pay, so sales of that number are suspended until the next draw. Tickets already sold remain guaranteed.
How long do I have to claim a Missouri Pick 3 prize?
180 days from the draw date. Prizes up to $600 can be claimed at any Missouri Lottery retailer; larger prizes go to headquarters in Jefferson City or regional offices in Kansas City (Independence), Springfield, or St. Louis, or by mail to Ticket Redemption, PO Box 7777, Jefferson City, MO 65102-7777.
How much tax does Missouri withhold on Pick 3 winnings?
Missouri's 4% state income tax applies to prizes over $600; federal withholding (24%) kicks in over $5,000. Most Pick 3 prizes fall under the state threshold — a $600 max Straight pays without state withholding — but all winnings are reportable income.
