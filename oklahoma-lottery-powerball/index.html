How to play Powerball in Oklahoma

Same rules as every MUSL state — played at any authorized Oklahoma Lottery retailer, from Oklahoma City and Tulsa to Norman, Broken Arrow and small-town party stores.

  1. Get a playslip at an authorized retailer.

    Oklahoma doesn't sell lottery tickets online or through apps — every Powerball ticket is purchased in person. You must be 18 or older.

  2. Pick 5 white balls (1–69) and 1 red Powerball (1–26).

    Choose your own numbers or ask for Easy Pick and let the terminal randomize them. Each play is $2.

  3. Optionally add Power Play for $1 more.

    Power Play multiplies most non-jackpot prizes by 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x or 10x (10x only when the advertised jackpot is $150 million or less). A $2 play becomes $3 with Power Play. The match-5 prize is guaranteed $2 million with Power Play instead of $1 million.

  4. Watch the drawing — Monday, Wednesday & Saturday.

    Drawings happen at 9:59 p.m. CT. Ticket sales close at 8:59 p.m. CT on draw nights, so buy at least an hour early. Results post on the Lottery's website right after the draw.

  5. Check your ticket — then sign it.

    Scan it at the retailer or check numbers online. If you've won, sign the back immediately: a signed ticket is the only thing that stops someone else from cashing it.

💡
Free second chances: mark your playslip for Oklahoma Lottery promotions when offered — non-winning tickets sometimes feed into second-chance and promotional drawings announced on oklottery.com.

How to claim a Powerball prize in Oklahoma

The one deadline that matters: 180 days from the drawing. Miss it and the money leaves for good.

⚠️
Unclaimed prizes don't roll into the next jackpot. Under Oklahoma law, unclaimed prize money is transferred to the Oklahoma Education Lottery Trust Fund — the state's public-education fund created by State Questions 705 and 706 in 2004.

Before you claim a big one, protect it: sign the ticket, photograph both sides, make a copy, and store the original somewhere flat, dry and boring. If you're ever in a dispute over a ticket — misprint, validation error, retailer scan mismatch — the paper trail is everything. Oklahoma Powerball jackpot history

Oklahoma joined Powerball in 2006 — and needed barely a month for its first jackpot: $101 million in June 2006. The state's record came a year later, on the June 27, 2007 drawing: a $105.8 million (annuitized) ticket bought in Roland, Sequoyah County, claimed by Don and Joyce Harvey of Muldrow. They chose the cash option — about $33.7 million after withholdings.

Since then, Oklahoma has kept feeding the winner lists with match-5 $1 million prizes and smaller tiers, sold everywhere from Oklahoma City and Tulsa metros to single-stop towns. The Oklahoma Lottery itself launched ticket sales on October 12, 2005, as a member of the Multi-State Lottery Association (MUSL), with proceeds dedicated to education.

Oklahoma Lottery Powerball — FAQ

What time is the Oklahoma Lottery Powerball drawing?
Powerball drawings are held Monday, Wednesday and Saturday at 9:59 p.m. Central Time. Oklahoma ticket sales close at 8:59 p.m. CT on draw nights, so buy your ticket at least an hour before the drawing.
How much does a Powerball ticket cost in Oklahoma?
A Powerball play costs $2 in Oklahoma. Adding Power Play — which multiplies non-jackpot prizes by 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x or 10x — costs $1 more per play, for $3 total.
Can I buy Powerball tickets online in Oklahoma?
No. Oklahoma does not sell lottery tickets online. Powerball tickets must be bought in person at an authorized Oklahoma Lottery retailer — gas stations, convenience stores and supermarkets across the state.
What are the odds of winning the Oklahoma Powerball jackpot?
The jackpot odds are 1 in 292,201,338. Matching all five white balls without the Powerball wins a fixed $1 million at odds of 1 in 11,688,054. The overall odds of winning any prize are about 1 in 24.9.
How long do I have to claim a Powerball prize in Oklahoma?
Powerball prizes in Oklahoma must be claimed within 180 days of the drawing date. (In-house games Pick 3 and Cash 5 have a shorter 90-day window.) Unclaimed prize money is transferred to the Oklahoma Education Lottery Trust Fund.
How much tax is withheld on an Oklahoma Powerball prize?
The Lottery automatically withholds 24% federal tax plus 4% Oklahoma state tax on prizes over $5,000. On a $1 million match-5 prize, that is $240,000 federal and $40,000 state — roughly $720,000 take-home before your final tax filing.
Has anyone in Oklahoma ever won the Powerball jackpot?
Yes. Oklahoma's first Powerball jackpot was $101 million in June 2006. The state's largest Powerball win to date is $105.8 million (annuitized), bought in Roland, Sequoyah County, and claimed by Don and Joyce Harvey of Muldrow, who took the cash option of about $33.7 million after withholdings.
