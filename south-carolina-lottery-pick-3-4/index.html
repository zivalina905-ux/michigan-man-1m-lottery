South Carolina Pick 3 & 4 — quick answer

Quick answer SC Pick 3: three digits 0–9, 50¢ or $1 a bet, $500 on a $1 Straight, $160/$80 Box, $60 Pairs — drawn 12:59 p.m. Midday (Mon–Sat) and 6:59 p.m. Evening (daily). SC Pick 4: four digits, $5,000 on a $1 Straight, $1,200/$800/$400/$200 Box, Combo plays to $24. Both support Fireball (doubles cost, adds a fifth swap digit). Claims: prizes ≤$500 at retailers; over $500 by mail with claim form + ID; 180-day deadline; SC taxes winnings at 7%.

How to play SC Pick 3 & Pick 4

  1. Pick your game and digits.

    Pick 3: three digits 0–9. Pick 4: four digits. Duplicates and leading zeros count — 0-0-7-7 and 1-1-1-2 are valid plays. Quick Pick available.

  2. Choose a play type.

    Pick 3: Straight, Box, Straight/Box, Combo, Front Pair, Back Pair. Pick 4: Straight, Box (4/6/12/24-way), Straight/Box, Combo (4/6/12/24-way).

  3. Bet 50¢ or $1.

    Straight/Box is a fixed $1. Combo costs the sum of its Straight entries — $2 to $24 depending on way-count and wager.

  4. Optionally add Fireball.

    Doubles the wager and draws a fifth swap digit — full detail below.

  5. Check and sign immediately.

    The printed ticket is the only valid entry. Sign the back — an unsigned Pick ticket is a bearer instrument.

SC Fireball: extra combinations on both games

Fireball doubles the wager and draws a fifth digit (0–9) after the main numbers. Fireball players may replace any one drawn digit with the Fireball — each possible replacement is a new winning combination. Worked example: play 4619 Straight; the draw is 4610, Fireball 9 — swap the 0 for the 9 and 4619 hits.

Combo plays: the any-order full-prize bet

  • Pick 3 Combo: 3-way costs $3, 6-way costs $6 per $1 unit — every arrangement is a separate Straight, so any order wins the full $500 per $1.
  • Pick 4 Combo: 4-way $4, 6-way $6, 12-way $12, 24-way $24 per $1 unit — same rule, scaled to four digits. The 24-Way Combo is the largest single-ticket bet SC Pick 4 offers.
  • When it beats Box: if you'd have played Box anyway and your digits hit, Combo returns the full Straight prize instead of the discounted Box figure. The price premium is the trade. Check the official South Carolina Pick 4 odds table on sceducationlottery.com for the full Combo breakdown.

South Carolina lottery Pick 3 & 4 — FAQ

What time is the South Carolina Pick 3 & 4 drawing?
SC Pick 3 and Pick 4 both draw twice daily: a Midday drawing at 12:59 p.m. (every day except Sundays and Christmas Day) and an Evening drawing at 6:59 p.m. every day. Ticket sales pause at 12:45 p.m. for Midday and 6:45 p.m. for Evening. The same schedule covers both games.
How much does South Carolina Pick 4 pay?
A $1 Straight Pick 4 bet pays $5,000 for matching all four digits in exact order ($2,500 on 50 cents). Box prizes scale by arrangement: $1,200 for a 4-way, $800 for a 6-way, $400 for a 12-way, and $200 for a 24-way match on a $1 play. Combo plays pay the full $5,000 per $1 unit regardless of order.
How does the Fireball work in SC Pick 3 and Pick 4?
Fireball is an optional add-on that doubles the wager. A fifth number from 0-9 is drawn, and Fireball players may replace any one drawn digit with it — creating extra winning combinations. Example: play 4619, draw is 4610 with Fireball 9 — swap the 0 for the 9 and you win.
What are the odds of winning SC Pick 3 and Pick 4?
Pick 3 Straight carries 1-in-1,000 odds for a $500 prize on $1. Pick 4 Straight carries 1-in-10,000 odds for $5,000. Box odds are easier: 1 in 333 (3-way) and 1 in 167 (6-way) on Pick 3; 1 in 2,500 down to 1 in 416 on Pick 4.
How much is South Carolina lottery tax on winnings?
South Carolina taxes lottery winnings at 7% — one of the higher state rates. Prizes $500 or under can be claimed at any SC retailer; prizes over $500 require a claim form with photo ID, mailed to SC Education Lottery, P.O. Box 11039, Columbia, SC 29211-1039. Federal withholding (24%) also applies over $5,000.
How long do I have to claim a SC Pick 3 or Pick 4 prize?
180 days from the draw date — the same window that applies to all South Carolina Education Lottery draw games. Tickets expire after that; unclaimed money goes to education funding.
Can I play SC Pick 3 and Pick 4 online?
Yes — the South Carolina Education Lottery supports online play, and winning tickets played online are credited to your account automatically. In-store play remains available at retailers statewide. Players must be 18 or older.
