Pennsylvania Pick 4 — quick answer

Quick answer Pennsylvania Pick 4 is a twice-daily draw game: Day at 1:35 p.m. ET (sales close 1:05 p.m.) and Evening at 6:59 p.m. ET (sales close 6:57 p.m.). Pick four digits 0–9, bet 50¢ or $1, and win with fixed prizes — $5,000 top prize on a $1 Straight (1 in 10,000). The Wild Ball add-on doubles your cost and draws a fifth number that can replace any drawn digit, multiplying your winning combinations. Claims run one year; prizes up to $2,500 pay at retailers, larger ones at Lottery offices or by mail. PA taxes lottery winnings at just 3.07% state (auto-withheld over $5,000) plus 24% federal. Two details worth knowing about PA pick 4 drawing time quirks:

  • The Evening cutoff is unusually tight — 6:57 p.m. for a 6:59 p.m. draw. Don't arrive at 6:58 expecting to play tonight.
  • Tickets bought after a cutoff aren't void — they play the next drawing automatically.
  • The draws use a random number generator, not physical ball machines — same odds, faster results. Pennsylvania Lottery Pick 4 results post on palottery.pa.gov minutes after each drawing, where certified numbers stay archived.

Compare with other states we've covered: Maryland's Pick games draw at 12:27 p.m. Midday, and South Carolina's Pick 3 runs 12:59/6:59 — Pennsylvania's 1:35/6:59 pair sits between them.

How to play PA Lottery Pick 4

  1. Pick four digits, 0–9.

    Duplicates and leading zeros count — 0-0-7-7 and 1-1-1-2 are valid plays. Or ask for a Quick Pick.

  2. Choose a play type.

    Straight (exact order), Box (any order — 4/6/12/24-Way depending on repeated digits), or Straight/Box (both on one $1 ticket).

  3. Bet 50¢ or $1.

    $1 bets pay exactly double the 50¢ prizes. Straight/Box plays are fixed $1.

  4. Add Wild Ball (optional).

    Doubles the ticket cost; adds the fifth-number swap mechanic explained below.

  5. Choose Day, Evening, or advance play.

    The playslip's "Number of Draws" section covers the next draw plus 2–7 future drawings on one purchase.

  6. Check and sign your ticket.

    Verify the printed digits, play type, session and Wild Ball option before leaving the counter — then sign the back.

📱
Online option: Pennsylvania sells draw games through its official iLottery platform — and digital tickets can't be lost, damaged, or stolen, which matters because a paper ticket must be physically presented to claim.

Wild Ball: Pennsylvania's extra-chances add-on

Wild Ball is PA's answer to other states' Fireball — with a twist in how it pays. PA Pick 4 Straight/Box players can add it too. Mechanics:

  • Every drawing produces a fifth number, the Wild Ball, from 0–9 alongside the main four.
  • Players who added Wild Ball may swap any one of the four drawn numbers for the Wild Ball number — each possible swap is a new combination checked against your ticket.
  • Cost doubles ($1 becomes $2, 50¢ becomes $1) — but the winning combinations more than double.

How to read it: the base prize pays when your ticket wins without needing the swap; the Wild Ball prize pays when a Wild Ball swap creates the win. A $1 Straight ticket with Wild Ball costs $2 total and can win $5,000 (clean), $1,500 (via swap), or nothing — versus just the $5,000-or-nothing without the add-on. More ways to win, smaller side-prizes: that's the trade.

PA Lottery Pick 4 — FAQ

What time is the PA Lottery Pick 4 drawing?
PA Pick 4 has two daily drawings: the Day drawing at 1:35 p.m. ET (sales close 1:05 p.m.) and the Evening drawing at 6:59 p.m. ET (sales close 6:57 p.m.). Tickets bought after a cutoff play the next drawing. Drawings use a random number generator rather than physical balls.
How much does PA Lottery Pick 4 pay?
The top prize is $5,000 for a $1 Straight play matching all four digits in exact order ($2,500 on a 50-cent play). Box prizes scale by the number of digit arrangements: $1,198 for a 4-Way Box down to $200 for a 24-Way Box on a $1 play. All tiers are fixed prizes.
What are the odds of winning PA Pick 4?
A Straight play carries 1-in-10,000 odds. Box plays are easier: 1 in 2,500 for a 4-Way down to 1 in 416 for a 24-Way. The best odds on the ticket come from the Wild Ball add-on — a Straight with Wild Ball wins at 1 in 2,500, and the overall best single-tier odds reach 1 in 104.
How does the Wild Ball work in PA Pick 4?
Wild Ball is an optional add-on that doubles the ticket cost. A fifth number from 0-9 is drawn with the main four, and Wild Ball players may swap any one of the drawn numbers for the Wild Ball — creating extra winning combinations. A $1 Straight with Wild Ball pays $1,500 on a Wild Ball match, versus $5,000 for the clean Straight.
How long do I have to claim a PA Pick 4 prize?
One year from the drawing date — the standard deadline for all Pennsylvania draw games. Prizes up to $2,500 can be paid at retailers (cash availability permitting), $2,500.01 and up must be claimed directly from the Pennsylvania Lottery in person or by mail to ATTN: CLAIMS, PO Box 8671, Harrisburg, PA 17105-8671.
How is a PA Pick 4 prize taxed?
Prizes over $600 are taxable. Pennsylvania automatically withholds its 3.07% personal income tax on prizes over $5,000, plus 24% federal withholding. Winners over $600 in a calendar year receive a W2-G form. Pennsylvania is one of the lowest-taxed lottery states.
Can I play PA Pick 4 online or in advance?
Advance play is built into the playslip: the Number of Draws section covers the next draw plus 2 to 7 additional future drawings. Pennsylvania also sells draw games online through its official iLottery platform, where digital tickets can't be lost, damaged or stolen.
