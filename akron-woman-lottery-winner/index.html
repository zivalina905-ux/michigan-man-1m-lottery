Annuity vs cash: the Bonus Play Millions fork

Bonus Play Millions' top prize is a genuine fork: $40,000 a year for 25 years ($1M total) or a $500,000 cash option. The Akron winner took the cash — halving the nominal total for immediate liquidity.

  • The cash case: $500,000 invested at 5% generates ~$25,000/year indefinitely while the principal grows; at Ohio's cost of living, it's a house plus a substantial investment portfolio at 25.
  • The annuity case: $40,000/year is guaranteed for 25 years — crash-proof, scam-resistant, no discipline required. Nominal total: $1 million.
  • The real variable: behavior. The full analysis of this exact trade-off — with the viral counter-math — is in our Brenda Aubin-Vega annuity-vs-lump-sum breakdown.

Ohio adds one deadline inside the deadline: a jackpot's cash option must be elected within 60 days of claiming the prize. Miss it and the annuity is automatic.
