How to play SC lottery Pick 3

Played at any authorized South Carolina Education Lottery retailer — from Charleston and Columbia to Greenville mom-and-pop stores. Tickets are in-person only; you must be 18+.

  1. Fill out a playslip or ask for Quick Pick.

    Choose three digits from 0 to 9 (each can repeat — 7-7-7 is a valid play), or let the terminal randomize them for you.

  2. Choose your play type.

    Straight, Box, Straight/Box, Combo, or Pairs (Front Pair / Back Pair). Each has its own odds and fixed prize — the full breakdown is below.

  3. Set your bet: $0.50 or $1.

    A $1 Straight pays $500; a 50-cent Straight pays $250. Straight/Box is a fixed $1. Add Fireball and the cost doubles.

  4. Pick Midday, Evening, or both.

    Play up to 14 consecutive drawings of one draw, or 28 when combining Midday and Evening — Advance Play lets you target a specific future draw too.

  5. Watch the drawing and check your ticket.

    Results post on sceducationlottery.com right after each draw. All Pick 3 prizes are fixed — no pooling, no splitting: if you match, the advertised amount is yours in full.

↩️
Safety net: bought the wrong numbers? A sale can be canceled within 20 minutes of purchase — but not after sales are suspended for that drawing (12:45 p.m. / 6:45 p.m.). Check the ticket before you leave the counter.

The five SC Pick 3 play types, explained

Pick 3's flexibility is the whole point — you're choosing how risky you want to be, not just which digits to play:

  • Straight — match all three digits in exact order. Hardest to hit, biggest prize: $500 on a $1 play.
  • Box — match all three digits in any order. A 3-Way Box means two digits are the same (2-2-3 style, 1-in-333 odds, $80 on $1... correction below); a 6-Way Box means three different digits (1-2-3 style, 1-in-167 odds). Fewer unique combinations = bigger Box prize.
  • Straight/Box — a $1 ticket that plays both: exact order wins the Straight prize plus the Box prize; any other order still wins the Box part. Best "middle ground" ticket in the game.
  • Combo — every Straight combination of your digits as separate $0.50 Straight plays, so any order wins the full Straight prize. Costs $1.50 (3-Way) to $3 (6-Way) per $0.50 unit.
  • Pairs (Front Pair / Back Pair) — match just the first two or last two digits in order. The easiest win in the game: 1 in 100, $50 on a $1 play.

Pick 3 Fireball: the $500-second-chance add-on

Fireball doubles the cost of any Pick 3 play — and adds a fourth drawn digit (0–9). When results come in, the Fireball can replace any one of the three drawn numbers, and each possible replacement is checked against your ticket. One near-miss ticket can become several winning combinations:

  • Play 1-2-3 Straight, draw is 1-2-9, Fireball is 3 → the 9 becomes 3: you win Straight $500 on a $1+Fireball play... adjusted to the official table below.
  • Fireball Straight payouts: 3 different digits (1 combination) $250; 2 like digits (1 combination) $90; 3 identical (3 combinations) $270.
  • Fireball Box payouts: up to $90 (3-Way, 3 winning combinations) and $30 per combination (6-Way).
  • Fireball Pairs: 1 matching combination pays $9, 2 like digits with 3 combinations pay $27.
  • Best single Fireball hit: $540 on a $2 Straight/Combo play with 3 identical digits.

The math in one sentence: Fireball roughly doubles your cost but multiplies your winning combinations — you're buying insurance against "so close" tickets. Whether that trade suits you depends on budget; the base game's 1-in-1,000 Straight odds are already the honest, published truth.

Smart-play notes for SC Pick 3

  • Fixed prizes cut both ways. No jackpot fatigue: your $500 Straight pays $500 whether you're the only winner or one of forty. That certainty is rare in lottery games.
  • Match play type to goal. Chasing $500? Straight. Want frequent small hits? Pairs at 1-in-100. Middle ground? Straight/Box covers both outcomes on one $1 ticket.
  • Dates are popular, pairs of like digits are 3-Way. If your digits contain two identical numbers (12-12 style dates), remember your Box is a 3-Way — lower hit-chance per draw but a bigger fixed prize.
  • Cancel window is real money. Twenty minutes to undo a mistake. After sales pause, the ticket plays whether you meant it or not.
  • Keep it entertainment. Pick 3's daily cadence is designed for routine play — budget a fixed amount, and if it stops being fun, South Carolina's gambling helpline is below.

SC lottery Pick 3 — FAQ

What time is the SC Pick 3 drawing?
The South Carolina Education Lottery holds a Midday Pick 3 drawing at 12:59 p.m. and an Evening drawing at 6:59 p.m. Midday draws run Monday through Saturday only — there is no Midday draw on Sundays or Christmas Day, while the Evening draw happens every day. Ticket sales pause at 12:45 p.m. and 6:45 p.m. before each drawing.
How much does SC Pick 3 pay?
The top prize is $500 for a $1 Straight play matching all three digits in exact order ($250 on a 50-cent play). All Pick 3 prizes are fixed, so every winner is guaranteed the full advertised amount. Pairs pay $50 on a $1 play, Box pays $160 (3-Way) or $80 (6-Way) on a $1 play, and Straight/Box pays up to $330 combined.
What are the odds of winning SC Pick 3?
Straight plays carry 1-in-1,000 odds. Box plays are easier: 1 in 333 for a 3-Way and 1 in 167 for a 6-Way. Pairs offers the best odds of any play type at 1 in 100 for a $50 prize on a $1 play.
How does Fireball work in South Carolina Pick 3?
Fireball is an optional add-on that doubles the cost of a play. A fourth digit from 0 to 9 is drawn, and you may replace any one of the three drawn numbers with the Fireball number — potentially turning a near-miss into a win, with multiple winning combinations possible on a single ticket.
How do I claim a Pick 3 prize in South Carolina?
Because Pick 3 tops out at $500, any South Carolina lottery retailer can pay a winning ticket on the spot. You can also visit the Columbia Claims Center or mail winning tickets to SC Education Lottery, P.O. Box 11039, Columbia, SC 29211-1039. The claim deadline is 180 days from the draw date.
Can I play SC Pick 3 online?
No. South Carolina sells Pick 3 tickets in person at authorized retailers only. You can, however, check results and use a number checker on the Lottery's official website after each Midday or Evening drawing.
How many drawings can I enter at once?
Up to 14 consecutive Midday or Evening drawings, or 28 drawings if you play both — plus an Advance Play option for entering a specific future draw. And if you buy the wrong ticket, you can cancel a sale within 20 minutes of purchase, as long as sales have not yet been suspended for that drawing.

🔞 Play responsibly. Must be 18+ in South Carolina. Gambling problem? Call 1-877-452-9910 — South Carolina's gambling addiction help line.
